Dual monoblock · hybrid integrated

HT-120

The warmth of tubes,
held in check.

A dual monoblock design. Two 6SL7 valves operate in Class A through a custom SRPP stage, feeding a bipolar Class-AB output. Two independent 300-watt toroidal transformers, with separately stabilised heater and rail voltages.

One hundred and twenty watts a side into eight ohms, one hundred and eighty into four. Jantzen Audio coupling throughout, a DC blocker on the mains, and twenty-five kilograms of chassis holding it all still.

About us

We started with
guitar amplifiers.

OSV Audio was established in 2012 by a group of engineers in Katowice, Poland. For the first years we traded as JK Amplifiers, building guitar and bass amplifiers — work that earned Gear Of The Year 2019 from Bass Player Magazine.

That is why an OSV sounds the way it does. Musicians notice immediately when an instrument stops sounding like itself, and years of building for them is a demanding education in what an amplifier may and may not do to a signal.

Alongside the instrument work we were developing Hi-Fi amplification, which met a warm reception from listeners and retailers alike. Today the company operates under the OSV Audio name, continuing the tradition of offering some of the finest Hi-Fi gear in a diverse and competitive market.

Everything is still made in one workshop in Myslowice, by hand, one unit at a time.

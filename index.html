The amplifiers

Two machines.
One idea.

A valve front end for the tone, a bipolar output stage for the control a loudspeaker actually needs. The difference between them is authority, not character.

HT-50

Hybrid integrated · 2 × 50 W

One JJ 6SL7 and two NOS 6J9P-E triodes in Class A, through a custom SRPP stage. Fifteen kilograms. The one the press has spent three years praising.

HT-120

Dual monoblock · 2 × 120 W

Two 6SL7 valves in Class A, two independent 300VA toroidal transformers, and 180 watts a side into four ohms. Twenty-five kilograms of it.

The craft

Chosen, not sourced.

I.

Components

Jantzen Audio Superior Z-Cap coupling capacitors. NOS valves, matched by ear. Toroidal transformers wound to specification. Components are selected for how they sound, not what they cost.

II.

Assembly

No production line, no batch of a hundred. Each chassis is built by one person and carries the hours that went into it. The mains supply is filtered by a DC blocker before it reaches anything that matters.

III.

Approval

Every unit is bench-tested against specification, run in, and then listened to on reference loudspeakers before it is allowed to leave the workshop.
